# Case study: a bakery landing page with order-ahead

*Portfolio sample. The bakery, prices and details are made up; everything listed under "What it does" and "Proof" is real and tested.*

**Example scenario.** A small bakery sells out by early afternoon. Regulars ask to have loaves kept aside, but requests arrive by phone and social media messages and get lost. The owner wants one simple page that shows what's baked and lets people order ahead.

**What I built.**
- A one-page site with today's bakes and prices, the bakery's story, opening hours and a clear "Order ahead" button.
- An order form that checks each order before accepting it, and explains any problem in plain English.
- A warm, homely look with a friendly serif, built mobile-first.

**What it does.**
- Refuses closed days, past days and days too far ahead, and makes sure each order says what the customer wants.
- Uses the bakery's own time zone, so "today" is always the bakery's today.
- Keeps everything the customer typed when something needs fixing.
- Works on phones, with keyboards and screen readers, and even with JavaScript turned off.
- Blocks simple spam bots without making people solve a puzzle.

**Proof.** 75 automated tests. A script plants 15 deliberate bugs, one at a time, and all 15 are caught. Checked in a real browser at laptop and phone sizes, with and without JavaScript.

**Stack.** Next.js, React, TypeScript, Vitest. Comes with a README and setup steps.
